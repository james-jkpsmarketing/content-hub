Step 1 of 2

Turn your Excel file into site data

Drop your latest JKPS_Content_Production.xlsx below. Everything happens in this browser: the file is not uploaded anywhere. You'll get a file called content.js.

Only the public parts are kept: ideas and their status, campaigns, scheduled posts and views, store info. Costs and rates, sessions, footage, the assets log, idea Notes and revenue/profit columns are left out. Save the workbook in Excel first so the formula results are current.

Step 2 of 2

Upload content.js to GitHub

  1. Go to your repository on github.com and open the data folder.
  2. Click Add file → Upload files and drag in the content.js you just downloaded. It replaces the old one.
  3. Click Commit changes. The live site updates in about a minute (refresh with Ctrl+F5 / Cmd+Shift+R).
